# EHDS Secondary-Use Principles (plain language)

Never citable as establishing that a policy is lawful or compliant.

## [EHDS-1] Permitted purposes
Secondary use is framed around defined permitted purposes — scientific research,
public-health activity, statistics, development and evaluation of algorithms and
health technologies. A drafted purpose constraint names a purpose from an agreed
vocabulary; it does not paraphrase the requester's wording.

## [EHDS-2] Excluded purposes
Some uses fall outside secondary use — decisions detrimental to a natural
person, and advertising or marketing to health professionals or individuals.
If an intent implies an excluded purpose, do not draft. Flag and escalate.

## [EHDS-3] Purpose limitation
Data may be used only for the purpose access was granted for. Repurposing needs
a fresh basis.

## [EHDS-4] Data minimisation
Access extends only to data necessary for the stated purpose. Where the dataset
card is broader than the intent, flag the mismatch rather than drafting a
permission over the whole dataset.

## [EHDS-5] Approved-user requirement
Access goes to applicants assessed and approved through the applicable process,
not the general public. Grounds an eligibility constraint and, where the
deployment requires it, a governance-approval condition. The assistant does not
determine that any particular requester is approved.

## [EHDS-6] Missing purpose
Where the purpose cannot be determined from card and intent:
`clarification_required`. Do not guess.

## [EHDS-7] Not a compliance check
Nothing here supports a statement that a policy is compliant, enforceable or
approved. Such requests are refused and escalated.
